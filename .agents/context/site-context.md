# MyCalcFinance — Site Context

> **URL**: https://mycalcfinance.com
> **Stack**: Vite-React SPA / Next.js + Vercel
> **Revenue**: AdSense
> **Core Value**: Free finance calculators — mortgage, loans, savings, credit cards, retirement. Instant results, no signup.

---

## 🎤 Brand Voice

- **Tone**: Trustworthy, educational, empowering. Like a financial advisor for everyone.
- **Persona**: A personal finance coach who makes money decisions less scary.
- **Style**: Show the formula, then a real-world example. Tables for product comparisons.
- **Address**: Second person ("you"). Audience: young professionals, homebuyers, first-time investors.
- **Values**: Financial literacy, privacy (no data stored), free tools, actionable advice.
- **Differentiator**: Always include a "What this means for you" section after calculations.
- **CTA Rule**: End every article with a CTA to the relevant calculator.

### Writing Rules
1. Always show the formula, then explain with a real-world example.
2. Use comparison tables for financial products (loans, investments, savings).
3. Avoid jargon — if you must use it (APR, amortization), define it immediately.
