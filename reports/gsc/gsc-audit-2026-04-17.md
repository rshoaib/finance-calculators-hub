### Finding: meta descriptions too long

Google typically truncates meta descriptions around 155–160 chars. **27 of 48** posts have `excerpt` exceeding 155 chars, meaning the SERP snippet gets cut mid-sentence. Worst offenders: `retirement-savings-by-age-benchmarks-2026` (212), `compound-interest-beginners-guide-2026` (204), `savings-goal-calculator-guide-2026` (203), `hsa-calculator-guide-2026` (191).

### Finding: generic twitter-card metadata

The rendered `<meta name="twitter:title">` / `<meta name="twitter:description">` on blog posts currently uses site-wide defaults ("MyCalcFinance — Free Financial Calculators" / "Free online financial calculators for mortgage, loans, compound interest, retirement, and taxes.") rather than the per-post values. `generateMetadata()` sets `openGraph.title/description` but not `twitter.title/description`, so Next.js falls back to the layout-level defaults.
