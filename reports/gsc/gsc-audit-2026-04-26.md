The three structural recommendations from last week's report remain open:

1. **Trailing-slash redirect cost.** Every internal `<a href>` points at the slug-without-trailing-slash form, then 308-redirects to the trailing-slash canonical. Single hop, not a chain — outside auto-fix scope — but cumulatively a measurable LCP/INP penalty on internal navigation.
