<h2>What Are Capital Gains?</h2><p>A capital gain is the profit you make when you sell an investment for more than you paid for it. The tax you owe depends on <strong>how long you held the asset</strong>.</p><p>Use our <a href="/capital-gains-tax-calculator">Capital Gains Tax Calculator</a> to compare your short-term vs long-term tax liability.</p><h2>Short-Term vs Long-Term</h2><h3>Short-Term Capital Gains</h3><p>Assets sold within 12 months are taxed as ordinary income — up to 37% for high earners.</p><h3>Long-Term Capital Gains</h3><p>Assets held over 12 months get preferential rates: 0%, 15%, or 20% depending on income.</p><h2>Strategies to Minimize Capital Gains Tax</h2><ul><li><strong>Hold for more than a year</strong> to qualify for lower long-term rates</li><li><strong>Tax-loss harvesting:</strong> Sell losing investments to offset gains</li><li><strong>Use tax-advantaged accounts</strong> like 401(k)s and IRAs</li><li><strong>Donate appreciated assets</strong> to avoid capital gains entirely</li></ul><p>Check your current bracket with our <a href="/tax-bracket-calculator">Tax Bracket Calculator</a>.
