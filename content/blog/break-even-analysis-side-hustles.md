<h2>What Is Break-Even Analysis?</h2><p>Break-even analysis answers: <strong>How many units do I need to sell to cover all my costs?</strong> At break-even, total revenue equals total costs.</p><p>Run your own numbers with our <a href="/break-even-calculator">Break-Even Calculator</a>.</p><h2>The Break-Even Formula</h2><p style="text-align:center;font-size:1.1em;font-weight:bold;color:#10b981;">Break-Even Units = Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit)</p><h2>Understanding Your Costs</h2><h3>Fixed Costs</h3><p>Website hosting, software subscriptions, equipment, insurance, rent.</p><h3>Variable Costs</h3><p>Materials, packaging, shipping, payment processing fees, marketplace fees.</p><h2>Example: Selling Candles on Etsy</h2><ul><li>Fixed costs: $150/month</li><li>Variable cost per candle: $8</li><li>Selling price: $24</li><li>Contribution margin: $16</li><li>Break-even: 10 candles/month</li></ul><h2>5 Ways to Lower Your Break-Even Point</h2><ol><li>Reduce fixed costs</li><li>Lower variable costs with bulk buying</li><li>Raise prices if your product delivers value</li><li>Bundle products for higher margins</li><li>Focus on high-margin items</li></ol><p>Track your overall finances with our <a href="/net-worth-calculator">Net Worth Calculator</a>.
